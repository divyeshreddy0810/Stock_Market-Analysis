## Technical Indicators Quick Guide

### Moving Averages (MA)
- **MA20**: Short-term trend
- **MA50**: Medium-term trend
- **MA200**: Long-term trend
- **Signal**: Price > MA20 > MA50 > MA200 = **BULLISH** ✅

### RSI (Relative Strength Index)
- **Range**: 0-100
- **>70**: Overbought (consider selling) ❌
- **<30**: Oversold (consider buying) ✅
- **50+**: Strong uptrend
- **50-**: Strong downtrend

### MACD
- **MACD > Signal Line**: Bullish ✅
- **MACD < Signal Line**: Bearish ❌
- **Histogram**: Shows momentum strength
- **Crossovers**: Key buy/sell signals

### Bollinger Bands
- **Upper Band**: Potential overbought level
- **Lower Band**: Potential oversold level
- **Tight Bands**: Expect breakout soon
- **Wide Bands**: High volatility

### ATR (Average True Range)
- **Rising ATR**: Volatility increasing
- **Falling ATR**: Market quieting down
- **Use**: Stop-loss = Entry ± (2 × ATR)

### Volatility
- **>3%**: High risk/reward
- **2-3%**: Normal
- **<2%**: Low risk, stable

## Trading Signals at a Glance

### 🟢 BUY Signals
- [ ] RSI < 30 (oversold)
- [ ] Price touches Bollinger Lower Band
- [ ] MACD crosses above Signal Line
- [ ] Price > 20-day MA
- [ ] High volume on green candle

### 🔴 SELL Signals
- [ ] RSI > 70 (overbought)
- [ ] Price touches Bollinger Upper Band
- [ ] MACD crosses below Signal Line
- [ ] Price < 20-day MA
- [ ] High volume on red candle

### ⏸️ HOLD Signals
- [ ] Price sideways between MAs
- [ ] RSI 40-60 (neutral)
- [ ] MACD not crossing
- [ ] Consolidation pattern forming

## Common Questions

### Q: Why compare normalized prices?
A: Stocks at different price levels (e.g., $5 vs $500) are hard to compare. Normalizing scales them 0-1 so you see movement patterns equally.

### Q: What's the difference between RSI and MACD?
A: RSI shows extremes (overbought/oversold), MACD shows trend changes. Use both together!

### Q: Which moving average should I follow?
A: MA200 for long-term, MA50 for medium-term, MA20 for short-term. Traders often use all three.

### Q: Is high volatility good or bad?
A: Neither! High volatility = more risk AND more opportunity. Good for traders, risky for long-term investors.

### Q: When is ATR useful?
A: Always! Use it to calculate stop-loss levels. Stop-loss = Entry Price ± (2 × ATR).

## Indicator Combinations for Different Strategies

### Conservative Strategy (Lower Risk)
1. Price above MA200 (long-term trend)
2. RSI 40-60 (not extreme)
3. MACD above Signal Line (momentum)
4. Low volatility (<2%)

### Aggressive Strategy (Higher Risk)
1. RSI > 50 and rising
2. MACD bullish crossover
3. Price near Bollinger Lower Band
4. High volume confirmation

### Swing Trading Strategy
1. RSI divergence (price ↑ but RSI ↓)
2. MACD histogram shrinking
3. Price bouncing off MA20
4. ATR increasing (breakout pending)

### Trend Following Strategy
1. Price > MA20 > MA50 > MA200
2. MACD above Signal Line
3. Volume increasing
4. RSI 50-70 (strong but not overbought)

## What Each Indicator Lacks

| Indicator | Weakness |
|-----------|----------|
| MA | Lags behind price movements |
| RSI | Can stay overbought/oversold for long |
| MACD | Less effective in sideways markets |
| Bollinger Bands | Many false signals at extremes |
| ATR | Only measures volatility, not direction |
| Volume | Can be manipulated |

**Solution**: Use multiple indicators together!

- **MACD**: 55-70% accuracy in trending markets
- **Moving Averages**: 50-65% accuracy for trend following
- **Bollinger Bands**: 40-60% accuracy at band extremes
- **Combined signals**: 60-75% accuracy overall

## Best Times to Use Indicators

| Indicator | Best Time | Avoid |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| MA | Trending markets | Sideways/flat |
| RSI | Overbought/oversold | During strong trends |
| MACD | Trend changes | Range-bound markets |
| Bollinger Bands | Volatility extremes | Quiet markets |
| ATR | Volatility assessment | All times (useful always) |

## Risk Management Tips

1. **Always use stop-losses** → Set at Entry ± (2 × ATR)
2. **Size your position** → Risk only 1-2% of portfolio per trade
3. **Diversify** → Compare multiple stocks before deciding
4. **Don't fight the trend** → Follow MA200 direction
5. **Confirm signals** → Wait for 2-3 indicators to align
6. **Watch volume** → Trust moves with high volume
7. **Use statistics** → Check volatility before trading
